Goal and durable architecture
-----------------------------
* Six logical sprites may use either TIA player. One/two-way overlap is solid;
  3+-way overlap uses persistent rotating two-of-N service.
* Non-overlapping logical sprites may reuse a physical player later in the frame.
* Raster is 192 visible lines / 96 two-line logical bands; public Y is 0..89 and
  X is 0..159.
* Scheduling occurs during VBLANK with bounded event ordering. P0 and P1 have
  intentionally different physical setup lead times but symmetric logical
  assignment.
* Horizontal position uses the renderer-local 160-byte packed RESP/HMP table.
  Do not copy the maintained multisprite table; its HMOVE phase is different.
  Setup line B must begin directly with `STA HMOVE` unless the table is
  recalibrated in Stella.
* Same-lane reservations include attribute/setup/bitmap work; current safe reuse
  distances are 15 logical Y units for P0 and 12 for P1.
* Do not modify/replace `renderers/multisprite/`; it remains the maintained
  compatibility and timing oracle.
