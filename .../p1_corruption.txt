P1 diagnostic corruption -- resolved
====================================

Status
------
RESOLVED. The user's git bisect identified badpatch.patch / commit 4785eb36...
as the introduction point. The dense collision-icon rows made the later animated
TIA panel's P0 positioning land on a RESP0 phase that poisoned P0/P1 copy state
seen by the next frame's six-glyph detail raster.

Fix
---
The animated panel now delays only its P0 RESP0 strobe by one extra 6502 NOP.
To keep the visible P0 target at the original x=32, the positioning routine's
raw coordinate changes from 32 to 26 and continues to derive HMP0 from the
normal diagnostic_reposition_table. P1, missiles, Ball, collision glyph data,
and the reusable six_glyph_component are unchanged.

Regression
----------
test/vcs_diagnostic_cartridge_stella.pl checks the normally blank trailing
portion of the NTSC joystick P1 detail raster before the legacy live-row mask:

    --assert-dark-rect 178,122,209,126

This oracle caught the original corruption and rejected several cosmetic or
startup-sensitive false fixes. The final RESP-phase fix passes it while also
matching the pre-existing golden raster outside the intentionally masked live
controller-detail rows.

Validation
----------
- vcs_diagnostic_cartridge.pl passes.
- example_assembly_allowlist.pl passes with the reviewed diagnostic digest.
- Fresh NTSC joystick Stella runs repeatedly pass the P1 dark-rectangle oracle.
- All 12 Stella 7.0 NTSC/PAL/SECAM x joystick/paddle/keypad/driving reference
  cases pass exactly outside the existing live-detail mask.

The failed RAM-padding, blank-row, entry/exit rebase, NUSIZ, VDEL, HMOVE, and
private-renderer experiments are development history and are not part of the
maintained fix.
