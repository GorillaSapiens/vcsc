Current contract
----------------
* 192 visible NTSC lines, modeled as 96 two-scanline logical bands.
* Six eight-row logical sprites may use P0 or P1.
* Public X range is 0..159; public Y range is 0..95. Events whose setup would
  begin above the visible top are preloaded during VBLANK.
* The diagnostic playfield is 12 rows of 16 physical scanlines. It is dynamic,
  asymmetric, non-reflected PF0/PF1/PF2 data rather than a static symmetric
  backdrop.
* Per-event sprite colors are supported.
* Persistent fairness tracks every omitted sprite, then stable-partitions omitted
  IDs ahead of shown IDs for the next frame. Fairness must not be confused with
  avoidable scheduler omissions.
* Normal same-lane setup reservations are intentionally long: P0 15 logical
  bands, P1 12. Directional cross-lane setup hazards are real timing constraints.
  A retained-P0 path has one narrowly tested exact-gap exception; do not weaken
  constants globally to improve an allocator score.
* Retained-X continuation is experimental: equal-X vertically disjoint sprites
  may reuse a lane without repeating normal RESP/HMOVE work.
* The equal-X 12-band endpoint / 7+5 bridge geometry has a forced physical
  orientation: endpoints on P1, bridge on P0. The VBLANK oracle scans only the
  future priority suffix, tests +/-12 Y before X, and caches proven future P1
  endpoint bits in `setup_line[6]` until that byte is cleared for its sort
  sentinel role. Keep that rule and scratch lifetime; all 720 priority
  permutations are verified against the real renderer.
* P0 top-preload Y=89..91 has an extra predecessor state so P0/P1 first visible
  GRP lines match across Y=85..95. Re-run the lane-stability GRP oracle after any
  raster-state change.
* Current `VCS_NTSC_EXTENDED_VBLANK` TIM64T split is **63/13**. Preserve the
  total blanking budget and this split until a deterministic timing witness proves
  another division is safe.
* Hand-written conditional branches in this renderer require explicit timing
  contracts; do not introduce `.flex` conditionals into beam/deadline paths.

Timing status
-------------
The state-dependent 262/263 Stella-frame jitter now has a permanent real-6502
regression. Its cause was VBLANK cost, not visible-raster control flow: repeated
bridge-orientation searches became pathological when many sprites shared an X
column. The repaired oracle limits partner work to later priority ranks and
performs the rare +/-12 Y test before any X comparison; a proven first endpoint
caches its future partner so priority order does not change bridge orientation.

The deterministic all-X witness passes 117 checked frames at the calibrated
262-line interval. Development validation also passed all 720 bridge priority
permutations and 252,200 held-layout checked frames with persistent priority
state across X palettes of 1, 2, 3, 4, 8, and 160 values, including shifted
same/near-column clusters. Keep the permanent witness even if future Monte Carlo
changes. Interactive Stella confirmation remains the next external check.

Allocator work still unfinished
--------------------------------
Even after frame timing is stable, the greedy lane allocator is not globally
optimal for every visible-occupancy<=2 layout. Retained-X short continuation and
bounded lane rebalancing are the intended mechanisms. Do not fake progress by
lowering the 15/12 reservation distances or broad directional hazard windows;
those shortcuts have already scheduled invisible sprites or lengthened frames.
