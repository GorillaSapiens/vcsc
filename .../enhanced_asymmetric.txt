Vertical contract
-----------------
* `lines` is required and currently accepts 192 or 228 active scanlines.
* 192 is 96 two-scanline logical bands, public Y=0..95, and is the NTSC
  diagnostic profile.
* 228 is 114 two-scanline logical bands, public Y=0..113, and is used natively
  by PAL/SECAM. It has no synthetic top/bottom visible padding.
* The asymmetric playfield is one logical row per two scanlines. PF0/PF1/PF2
  are independently supplied for left and right halves.
* The 228 profile packs left/right PF0 into one page-contained 228-byte table;
  the 192 profile retains separate page-contained 96-byte PF0 tables so its
  very tight unbanked 4K diagnostic remains linkable.
* The 192 NTSC `VCS_NTSC_EXTENDED_VBLANK` TIM64T split remains 63/13. Do not
  change the total blanking budget without a measured timing witness.

Scheduler/raster invariants
---------------------------
* Six eight-row logical sprites may use P0 or P1; per-event colors are
  supported.
* Public X remains 0..159.
* Ordinary same-lane setup reservations are P0=15 logical bands and P1=12.
  These are measured timing constraints, not allocator heuristics.
* Identical X with |delta-Y| < 13 cannot reuse the same hardware player.
  P0 retained-X is safe only at delta 13..14. P1 exact-delta-12 ordinary reuse
  is permitted only when X differs.
* If neither lane is safe, use persistent fair omission; do not weaken timing
  constraints merely to improve apparent occupancy.
* The retired equal-X delta-12 bridge must not return. Maintained boundary
  regressions require the unsafe/safe same-X threshold and complete glyphs.
* Events whose setup begins above the first visible band are preloaded during
  VBLANK; the top-preload constants are derived from `lines`.
* Hand-written conditional branches in beam/deadline paths require explicit
  timing contracts; do not introduce `.flex` there.
* Preserve the full asymmetric PF0/PF1/PF2 rewrite contract on every raster
  path, including position/setup bands.

Current RAM/ROM boundary
------------------------
The 192 public diagnostic intentionally remains an unbanked 4K cartridge with
very little ROM headroom. The native 228 PAL/SECAM examples use F8 because the
larger renderer/data profile exceeds that practical 4K boundary. The 228
profile spends six additional RIOT bytes on the sprite-bit table. The public
F8 PAL/SECAM examples currently use 123/128 RAM. Their visible-phase wrapper
enters the renderer bank while VBLANK is still asserted, then performs
`end_vblank()`, `draw()`, the component handoff, and `begin_overscan()` resident
in that bank. Never put an F8 cross-bank trampoline between `end_vblank()` and
`draw()`; its cycles make the first renderer WSYNC miss the intended boundary.
