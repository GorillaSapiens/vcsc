VCSC PAL / SECAM VIDEO-STANDARD -- DURABLE CLOSEOUT
==================================================

Status
------
No unfinished items. Completed implementation history is archived in
`.../context-history/`; the full pre-cleanup roadmap is in 2026-08-22.

Durable decisions
-----------------
* NTSC, PAL, and SECAM remain explicit target/display contracts.
* Every maintained frame generator uses an exact three-scanline VSYNC pulse:
  assertion and deassertion are at the same cycle phase, exactly 228 CPU cycles
  apart. Keep downstream phase compensation outside the asserted VSYNC interval.
* PAL and SECAM may share 50 Hz frame/timing infrastructure where the hardware
  timing contract is genuinely common, but their color/palette behavior remains
  separate.
* Visible scanline count belongs to the renderer/component contract. The
  official all-five renderer uses the compile-time `lines` parameter: 192 for
  the native NTSC field and 228 for the native PAL/SECAM field. Do not emulate a
  native full-height renderer by wrapping a shorter renderer in visible padding.
* A full-height 228-line component returns at cycle zero after its terminal
  WSYNC. PAL/SECAM callers use `component_to_overscan_handoff()` before
  `begin_overscan()` to restore the calibrated timer-entry phase; this bridge
  adds cycles, not visible scanlines.
* Renderer/example play targets must pass the intended `-tv` setting to Stella;
  do not infer correctness from a default emulator standard.
* Exact frame/raster regressions and the public video-standard examples are the
  authorities for behavior. Do not duplicate per-version test totals here.
