VCSC RENDERER / STRIPES ROADMAP
================================

Goal
----
Add compile-time PF/color stripes to `all_five`, then the remaining maintained
renderers, zero path.

Stripe ABI
----------
Add `parameter stripes := 0;`; zero preserves the old path with no arrays.
`stripes:=N` requires `game_playfield_colors[N*2]` and
`game_playfield_data[N*6]` (`TEMPLATE_playfield_colors/data`). Store COLUPF/COLUBK
then left/right PF0/PF1/PF2; stripe i owns
`floor(i*L/N)..floor((i+1)*L/N)-1`. Preserve raster/timing/handoffs/resources/write
phases; no runtime division or per-line feature test.

Positive stripes require a six-write PF0/PF1/PF2 schedule and must preserve the
maintained write-phase families, frame timing, object raster, handoffs, and zero
path. Completed timing measurements and discarded prototypes live in daily
history and executable regressions.

Renderer constraints
--------------------
Preserve existing frame, handoff, resource, branch, object, phase, composition,
and pinned-palette Stella contracts. Full-height 192/228 remains terminal-WSYNC
and score-composable 170/181 retains its entry/successor behavior.

Steps
-----
Completed: the 192-line `all_five` prototype has stable one-stripe timing,
double-buffer A/B PF storage, exact two-stripe data/color handoff, certified
intra-row tail-1 (98/94) and tail-2 (100/92) seams, generated coarse/tail ROM
descriptors, and runtime selection between tail 1 and tail 2 with no net beam
phase cost.  The selector is paid by precomputing the coarse-wrap P1 byte in
VBLANK; the visible GRP1 store stays at its original cycle.

Remaining work is deliberately flat:

1. Add and certify tail handlers 0 and 3..7, then extend runtime selection to
   all eight tail values without a per-pair tax.
2. Restore default autogenerated equal-height distribution through the generic
   coarse-row/tail scheduler; temporary 98/94 and 100/92 splits remain only
   certification fixtures.
3. Schedule inactive-buffer refill across the active stripe.
4. Measure refill service time and enforce the resulting minimum even stripe
   height at compile time.
5. Generalize the double-buffer scheduler to N > 2 and add asymmetric N=3 and N=5
   fixtures.
6. Add programmer-supplied height tables; reject odd heights, incorrect sums,
   and heights below the measured minimum.  Never silently round.
7. Certify 192-line stripes broadly: zero-path byte identity, resources,
   motion, branch contracts, visible handoff, RGB Stella references, and
   generated-height edge cases.
8. Certify every maintained line mode, including non-divisible 228 and the
   181/170 score-composable handoffs.
9. Exercise all supported missile/player-color combinations.
10. Choose a new public renderer name; `all_five` is only the working name and
    no longer describes the expanded renderer especially well.
11. Add a public example and documentation for geometry, storage, costs,
    composition, even-height rules, and minimum-height rules.
12. Add and certify stripes in `multisprite`.
13. Add and certify stripes in `enhanced_multisprite_asymmetric` for 192/228.
14. Close roadmap item 57 once obsolete renderers are gone and the normal suite
    is clean.
