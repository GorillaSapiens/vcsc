VCSC RENDERER / STRIPES ROADMAP
================================

Goal
----
Add compile-time PF/color stripes to `all_five`, then the remaining maintained
renderers, zero path.

Stripe ABI
----------
Add `parameter stripes := 0;`; zero preserves the old path with no arrays.
`stripes:=N` requires `game_playfield_colors[N*2]` and
`game_playfield_data[N*6]` (`TEMPLATE_playfield_colors/data`). Store COLUPF/COLUBK
then left/right PF0/PF1/PF2; stripe i owns
`floor(i*L/N)..floor((i+1)*L/N)-1`. Preserve raster/timing/handoffs/resources/write
phases; no runtime division or per-line feature test.

Positive stripes require a six-write PF0/PF1/PF2 schedule and must preserve the
maintained write-phase families, frame timing, object raster, handoffs, and zero
path. Completed timing measurements and discarded prototypes live in daily
history and executable regressions.

Renderer constraints
--------------------
Preserve existing frame, handoff, resource, branch, object, phase, composition,
and pinned-palette Stella contracts. Full-height 192/228 remains terminal-WSYNC
and score-composable 170/181 retains its entry/successor behavior.

Steps
-----
Completed: the 192-line `all_five` stripe kernel now has stable one-stripe
timing, double-buffer A/B PF storage, generated pair-height/coarse-tail ROM
descriptors, independently certified tail landings 0..7, and one runtime
all-eight tail dispatcher with no per-pair selection tax.  The default
`stripes:=2` build uses the same generic scheduler with autogenerated equal
48/48-pair heights (96/96 scanlines); the old 98/94 through 110/82 splits are
certification fixtures only.  A real refill probe also proves that six PF bytes
can be reconstructed from ROM into the inactive buffer in six two-line service
pairs with zero beam-time cost: the P1 selection slot is replaced by one
ROM-to-RAM copy and a cached GRP1 load; PF0 normalization fits in the same slot.
This establishes a raw refill service cost of 12 scanlines, but not yet the
public minimum height.

Remaining work is deliberately flat:

1. Finish generic inactive-buffer service-row scheduling.  The six-byte PF
   primitive is proven; what remains is one-shot placement for every boundary
   plus preparation of the future service row's cached P1 bytes without a
   per-pair tax or unbounded RAM.
2. Measure the complete service pipeline (PF refill plus any sprite-cache
   preparation/wait) and enforce the resulting minimum even stripe height at
   compile time.  Do not claim the raw 12-scanline PF-copy cost as the public
   minimum until the complete pipeline is scheduled.
3. Generalize the double-buffer scheduler to N > 2 and add asymmetric N=3 and
   N=5 fixtures.
4. Add programmer-supplied height tables; reject odd heights, incorrect sums,
   and heights below the measured minimum. Never silently round.
5. Certify 192-line stripes broadly: zero-path byte identity, resources,
   motion, branch contracts, visible handoff, RGB Stella references, and
   generated-height edge cases.
6. Certify every maintained line mode, including non-divisible 228 and the
   181/170 score-composable handoffs.
7. Exercise all supported missile/player-color combinations.
8. Choose a new public renderer name; `all_five` is only the working name and
   no longer describes the expanded renderer especially well.
9. Add a public example and documentation for geometry, storage, costs,
   composition, even-height rules, and minimum-height rules.
10. Add and certify stripes in `multisprite`.
11. Add and certify stripes in `enhanced_multisprite_asymmetric` for 192/228.
12. Close roadmap item 57 once obsolete renderers are gone and the normal suite
    is clean.
