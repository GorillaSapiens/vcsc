VCSC RENDERER / STRIPES ROADMAP
================================

Goal
----
Before item 57, consolidate duplicated gameplay renderers and components.
`all_five`, `player_color`, and both `all_five_player_color_*` profiles become
compile-time specializations selected by `lines`, missiles, player colors, and
later `stripes`. Retire both unofficial and both `standard_4k_ntsc*` legacy profiles after preserving useful coverage. `multisprite` and
`enhanced_multisprite_asymmetric` remain distinct. Move reusable components,
including `poison_debug_score`, under `libraries/vcs/components/`.

Stripe ABI
----------
Participating renderers gain `parameter stripes := 0;`. Zero preserves the old
path and needs no arrays. `stripes:=N` requires instance-qualified:

    game_playfield_colors[N * 2]
    game_playfield_data[N * 6]

Template names are `TEMPLATE_playfield_colors` and `TEMPLATE_playfield_data`.
Each stripe stores COLUPF/COLUBK, then left and right PF0/PF1/PF2 raw TIA bytes.
Stripe i owns `floor(i*L/N)` through `floor((i+1)*L/N)-1` of L visible lines.
Stripes are screen-space bands. Measure storage needs. Preserve raster, timing, handoffs, resources, and write phases.

Steps
-----
S1. Record this architecture, stripe ABI, and flat migration order.

S2. Create `libraries/vcs/components/`; move reusable components/support and
    `poison_debug_score`; update callers, manifests, tests, examples, and docs.

S3. Inventory the six modern gameplay and two legacy profiles: ABI, schedules,
    line modes, resources, examples, raster oracles, and tests.

S4. Establish one gameplay renderer based on `all_five` with compile-time
    line/missile/player-color selection and unchanged `all_five` behavior.

S5. Migrate `player_color` profiles and prove existing behavior/resources before
    deleting their old source.

S6. Migrate `all_five_player_color_192`; prove equivalence before deletion.

S7. Migrate `all_five_player_color_181`; preserve score-composable handoff and
    prove equivalence before deletion.

S8. Deduplicate shared ABI/positioning/playfield/setup while retaining genuinely
    different compile-time schedules.

S9. Retire both unofficial gameplay profiles after relocating useful coverage.

S10. Port useful `standard_4k_ntsc*` coverage, then delete both legacy profiles and
     their compatibility-only macros, inventory, tests, and docs.

S11. Normalize examples/tests/docs/manifests around the unified renderer; run broad regression before stripes.

S12. Build `lines:=192, stripes:=3` with distinct PF/BK colors and asymmetric
     left/right PF data; measure legal write slots and storage constraints.

S13. Implement stripes with an unchanged zero path and compile-time boundaries;
     no runtime division or per-line feature test.

S14. Certify 192-line stripes with API, TIA-phase, simulator/Stella raster,
     object-motion, and zero-path byte/resource compatibility tests.

S15. Extend/certify every supported line mode, including non-divisible 228 geometry
     and 181/170 score handoffs.

S16. Exercise stripes across supported missile/player-color combinations.

S17. Add a public example and document ABI, geometry, storage, costs, line modes, and composition.

S18. Add/certify the same stripe ABI in `multisprite`.

S19. Add/certify it in `enhanced_multisprite_asymmetric` for 192/228 modes.

S20. Close item 57 when obsolete renderers are gone, docs/manifests/examples are
     coherent, and the normal suite is clean.
