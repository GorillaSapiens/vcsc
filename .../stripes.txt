VCSC RENDERER / STRIPES ROADMAP
================================

Goal
----
Add compile-time PF/color stripes to `all_five`, then the remaining maintained
renderers, zero path.

Stripe ABI
----------
Add `parameter stripes := 0;`; zero preserves the old path with no arrays.
`stripes:=N` requires `game_playfield_colors[N*2]` and
`game_playfield_data[N*6]` (`TEMPLATE_playfield_colors/data`). Store COLUPF/COLUBK
then left/right PF0/PF1/PF2; stripe i owns
`floor(i*L/N)..floor((i+1)*L/N)-1`. Preserve raster/timing/handoffs/resources/write
phases; no runtime division or per-line feature test.

Positive stripes require a six-write PF0/PF1/PF2 schedule and must preserve the
maintained write-phase families, frame timing, object raster, handoffs, and zero
path. Completed timing measurements and discarded prototypes live in daily
history and executable regressions.

Renderer constraints
--------------------
Preserve existing frame, handoff, resource, branch, object, phase, composition,
and pinned-palette Stella contracts. Full-height 192/228 remains terminal-WSYNC
and score-composable 170/181 retains its entry/successor behavior.

Steps
-----
Completed: the 192-line kernel has stable `stripes:=1`, A/B PF buffers,
coarse/tail descriptors, certified tails 0..7, and a runtime all-eight dispatcher.  Default `stripes:=2` uses equal 48/48-pair heights; asymmetric
splits remain certification fixtures.  The refill probe reconstructs all six
inactive PF bytes in six beam-neutral pairs by storing the six service P1 bytes
in that same inactive buffer and overwriting each after use.  No third cache is
needed; RAM is 103 bytes.  The hot PF0 copy currently requires canonical PF0
source bytes (ignored low nibble zero); public ABI work must validate this or provide cheap normalization.  Raw PF service is 12 lines, not
yet the public minimum.

192-line certification is complete: zero-path identity, resources, generated-height edges, branch/handoff contracts, Stella RGB, and moving-P0 RESP/HMP. The future public renderer name is `gameplay`; the actual path/API rename waits until the stripe ABI is stable.

Remaining:

1. Finish generic inactive-buffer service-row scheduling.  The six-byte PF
   primitive is proven with self-hosted A/B storage; what remains is one-shot
   placement for every boundary plus rolling preparation of the next six P1
   service bytes without a per-pair tax or RAM that scales with stripe count.
   A cache-free scheduler using P1-inactive pairs is a candidate, but must not
   silently impose a new player-height restriction.
2. Measure the complete service pipeline (PF refill plus any sprite-cache
   preparation/wait) and enforce the resulting minimum even stripe height at
   compile time.  Do not claim the raw 12-scanline PF-copy cost as the public
   minimum until the complete pipeline is scheduled.
3. Generalize the double-buffer scheduler to N > 2 and add asymmetric N=3 and
   N=5 fixtures.
4. Add programmer-supplied height tables; reject odd heights, incorrect sums,
   and heights below the measured minimum. Never silently round.
5. Certify every maintained line mode, including non-divisible 228 and the
   181/170 score-composable handoffs.
6. Exercise all supported missile/player-color combinations.
7. Add a public example and documentation for geometry, storage, costs,
   composition, even-height rules, minimum-height rules, and the chosen
   `gameplay` name/API migration.
8. Add and certify stripes in `multisprite`.
9. Add and certify stripes in `enhanced_multisprite_asymmetric` for 192/228.
10. Close roadmap item 57 once obsolete renderers are gone and the normal suite
    is clean.
