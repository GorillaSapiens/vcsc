VCSC RENDERER / STRIPES ROADMAP
================================

Goal
----
Add compile-time PF/color stripes to `all_five`, then the remaining maintained
renderers, zero path.

Stripe ABI
----------
Add `parameter stripes := 0;`; zero preserves the old path with no arrays.
`stripes:=N` requires `game_playfield_colors[N*2]` and
`game_playfield_data[N*6]` (`TEMPLATE_playfield_colors/data`). Store COLUPF/COLUBK
then left/right PF0/PF1/PF2; stripe i owns
`floor(i*L/N)..floor((i+1)*L/N)-1`. Preserve raster/timing/handoffs/resources/write
phases; no runtime division or per-line feature test.

Positive stripes preserve the maintained frame, handoff, resource, branch,
object, phase, composition, zero-path, and pinned-palette Stella contracts.
192/228 remain terminal-WSYNC; 170/181 retain score-composable handoffs.
Measurements and discarded prototypes belong in tests or daily history.

Steps
-----
Completed: 192-line `stripes:=1/2`, A/B buffers, all tail handlers, runtime
dispatch, six-line PF refill, zero-path/resource/height/branch/handoff/motion
contracts, and Stella RGB are green. No third cache or stripe-count RAM is used;
canonical PF0 source bytes keep their low nibble zero for the hidden enable
baseline. Future public renderer name: `gameplay`; rename waits for stable ABI.

Remaining:

1. Finish rolling inactive-buffer scheduling around the authoritative
   visible machine in `stripe_pair_machine_search.pl`. One exact 152-cycle epoch
   runs from physical A:02 through the following A:01. `ordinary` and `refill`
   modes share all 17 TIA appointments. The machine composes directly: the
   three-pair refill form moves all six PF bytes plus both next colors in six
   scanlines without shifting a TIA event or adding scheduler idle. Pair 2 still
   exposes a real seven-cycle budget where the first two pairs copy colors.

   Refill works by replacing soft feed work: P1 uses the fixed 23-cycle feed
   slot, P0 tails use the fixed 16-cycle feed/carry slot, and preclassified P0
   bookkeeping plus a zero-page PF0 store makes a seven-cycle color copy fit in
   the existing B-line PF2L..PF0R interval. `stripe_pair_exact_search.pl` and
   `stripe_three_pair_exact_search.pl` are compatibility shims only.

   All visible pairs must keep this cadence. Do not add or certify a separately
   timed service row. Next spend the explicit pair-2 seven-cycle budget and/or
   preceding ordinary feed slots on rolling P0/P1 preparation, and account for
   the complete planner/dispatch cost in this same machine before production
   emission.
2. Measure the complete rolling service pipeline (including service-sprite and
   next-color preparation) and enforce the resulting minimum even stripe height.
   Six lines is now the proven PF-copy cost, not yet the public minimum.
3. Generalize the double-buffer scheduler to N > 2 and add asymmetric N=3 and
   N=5 fixtures.
4. Add programmer-supplied height tables; reject odd heights, incorrect sums,
   and heights below the measured minimum. Never silently round.
5. Certify every maintained line mode, including non-divisible 228 and the
   181/170 score-composable handoffs.
6. Exercise all supported missile/player-color combinations.
7. Add a public example and documentation for geometry, storage, costs,
   composition, even-height rules, minimum-height rules, and the chosen
   `gameplay` name/API migration.
8. Add and certify stripes in `multisprite`.
9. Add and certify stripes in `enhanced_multisprite_asymmetric` for 192/228.
10. Close roadmap item 57 once obsolete renderers are gone and the normal suite
    is clean.
