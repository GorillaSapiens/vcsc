VCSC RENDERER / STRIPES ROADMAP
================================

Goal
----
Add compile-time PF/color stripes to `all_five`, then the remaining maintained
renderers, zero path.

Stripe ABI
----------
Add `parameter stripes := 0;`; zero preserves the old path with no arrays.
`stripes:=N` requires `game_playfield_colors[N*2]` and
`game_playfield_data[N*6]` (`TEMPLATE_playfield_colors/data`). Store COLUPF/COLUBK
then left/right PF0/PF1/PF2; stripe i owns
`floor(i*L/N)..floor((i+1)*L/N)-1`. Preserve raster/timing/handoffs/resources/write
phases; no runtime division or per-line feature test.

Positive stripes require a six-write PF0/PF1/PF2 schedule and must preserve the
maintained write-phase families, frame timing, object raster, handoffs, and zero
path. Completed timing measurements and discarded prototypes live in daily
history and executable regressions.

Renderer constraints
--------------------
Preserve existing frame, handoff, resource, branch, object, phase, composition,
and pinned-palette Stella contracts. Full-height 192/228 remains terminal-WSYNC
and score-composable 170/181 retains its entry/successor behavior.

Steps
-----
Architecture: positive stripes double-buffer two six-byte PF images plus a
two-byte next-color latch. Refill the inactive side during the active stripe;
boundaries only switch buffer/colors. The two-line kernel requires even heights
and schedules in scanline pairs.

Height policy: initially generate pair heights by distributing L/2 pairs as
evenly as possible across N stripes. Later accept caller heights, rejecting odd
values, sums other than L, and heights below the measured refill minimum. Never
round.

S13c2c. Generalize the proven intra-row schedule per double-buffer segment.
Represent each pair height as coarse eight-pair rows plus a 0..7-pair tail. X is
the coarse-row counter. ROM descriptors and the current descriptor-derived X
seed are certified; VBLANK also precomputes the next stripe's X seed and tail
selector. A cross-inline-body indirect JMP prototype was rejected because it
breaks compiler reachability for the caller loop. Next implement fixed tail
landings with compiler-visible direct dispatch at the X-wrap seam, then certify
a second uneven split with a different tail. No per-pair countdown or runtime
division/modulo belongs in the visible hot path.

S13c2d. Restore the default autogenerated equal distribution and drive the same
coarse-row/tail scheduler from its pair-height table. The temporary 98/94
S13c2b split exists only to certify the intra-row mechanism; it is not the final
public `stripes=2` geometry.
S13d. Schedule inactive-buffer refill across the active stripe, measure the
number of scanline pairs required, and make that the compile-time minimum stripe
height.
S13e. Generalize the double-buffer scheduler to N>2 and add asymmetric N=3/N=5
fixtures that prove boundaries, colors, PF values, and frame timing.
S14. Certify 192 stripes broadly: zero-path byte identity, resources, motion,
branch contracts, visible handoff, exact RGB Stella references, and generated
height-table edge cases.
S15. Certify every line mode, including non-divisible 228 and 181/170 handoffs.
S16. Exercise supported missile/player-color combinations.
S17. Add public example/docs for geometry/storage/costs/composition and document
the even-height/minimum-height contract.
S18. Add/certify stripes in `multisprite`.
S19. Add/certify stripes in `enhanced_multisprite_asymmetric` 192/228.
S20. Close item 57 when obsolete renderers are gone and normal suite is clean.
