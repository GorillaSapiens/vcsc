2026-07-29 20:12:44 PDT, add the lone-underscore discard token
------------------------------------------------------------
* Have the lexer return a dedicated `DISCARD` token for the exact one-character
  spelling `_`; longer identifiers containing underscores remain ordinary
  identifiers.
* Add value-less simple-assignment forms in both directions. `lvalue := _`
  performs no source load or conversion and stores whatever byte is already in
  A; a direct one-byte absolute register such as `WSYNC` therefore lowers to
  one bare `STA`. `_ := expression` evaluates the expression normally for
  calls, assignments, and other effects without creating a destination object.
* Restrict assignment from discard to one-byte, non-bitfield lvalues. Reject
  wider and bitfield targets rather than inventing ambiguous multibyte or
  read-modify-write semantics. Both discard forms are intended as value-less
  expression statements rather than operands in larger expressions.
