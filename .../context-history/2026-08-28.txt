2026-08-28 17:49:14 PDT, document linker dummy-read hazard
The diagnostic P1 corruption is now attributed to NMOS 6502 page-cross dummy
reads from a wrapped absolute,Y reposition-table lookup. With the table linked
at $F14D the encoded base was $F05D; Y=$F3 and Y=$F1 reached final ROM addresses

corruption is caused by read bus cycles with destructive SC write-port side
effects. RIOT zero-page/absolute controls and a no-page-cross SC control rule
out the earlier timing and direct cartram-lowering theories.
