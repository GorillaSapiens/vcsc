ENHANCED ASYMMETRIC RETAINED-P0 FOLLOW-UP
==========================================

* Added main-roadmap Item 43, "Efficient startup for simple RAM setup", ahead
  of the previous roadmap items. It records that the compiler accepts only
  `void main(void);`, main is non-returning, erroneous fall-through/return uses
  `JMP ($FFFC)` rather than `RTS`, startup enters main with `JMP`, and the linker
  should automatically select the abbreviated RIOT-BSS-only startup when safe.
* Fixed one concrete retained-X dispatcher bug in
  `enhanced_multisprite_asymmetric/enhanced_multisprite.c26`: the final `LDX` in
  `ChooseLane` clobbered N after forming $80/$81, so the caller's `BMI` could
  never reliably dispatch retained lanes. The X load now occurs before the
  final A/flag-producing operations. This is byte/cycle-neutral.
* That fix exposed the next real renderer defect: retained P0 currently skips
  positioning but still traverses the ordinary P0 setup pipeline, producing
  stale/recolored GRP0 data and a vertically late successor bitmap. The dedicated
  retained-P0 graphics/color handoff requested by the user was not completed
  before the message-stream interruption.

RETAINED-P0 FIRST RASTER STEP
=============================

* Removed a redundant VBLANK `LDA #0`; A was already zero. This saves two ROM
  bytes and two VBLANK cycles.
* `Position0LineA` now publishes `current_gfx` to GRP0. Ordinary P0 publishes
  zero; retained P0 preserves the predecessor row. Matching `TAY`/padding
  reductions keep the established line phase unchanged.
* Focused asymmetric timing/visibility stress passes. The known equal-X trace
  restores the next predecessor row at line 86, proving the handoff direction.
* The handoff remains unfinished: successor COLUP0 is still installed too early
  and later predecessor rows are still missing. A pre-WSYNC trigger experiment
  caused 270 raw lines and was reverted; do not add cycles there. A naive
  P0RetainSlot advance overflowed the 4K image and was also discarded.
