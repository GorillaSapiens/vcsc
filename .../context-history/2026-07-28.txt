2026-07-28 21:22:49 PDT, lower BCD decimal-power multiplication division
* Added packed-BCD `*`, `/`, `%`, `*=`, `/=`, and `%=` support for positive
  integer constant expressions exactly equal to powers of ten across `bcd8_t`,
  `bcd16_t`, `bcd24_t`, and `bcd32_t`. Multiplication also accepts the constant
  on the left; division and remainder keep the conventional constant-right
  form. Other BCD multiply/divide/remainder operands remain rejected.
* Added a shared decimal-power classifier and inline packed-digit lowering.
  Even decimal shifts become byte moves and clears. Odd shifts use four `ASL`
  or `LSR` instructions with `$0f` masks and nibble joins. No `_mul8`..`_mul32`
  or `_div8`..`_div32` helper is selected, and generated code does not execute
  `SED` for these operations.
* Defined fixed-width behavior explicitly: multiplication drops high digits,
  division drops low digits, and remainder retains the low N decimal digits.
  Powers at least as wide as the type therefore produce zero for multiply and
  divide, while modulo returns the original value. `10^0` acts as identity for
  multiply/divide and produces zero remainder.
* Added `test/bcd_power_of_ten_codegen_test.c26` and
  `test/e2e_bcd_power_of_ten_verify.c26`, covering every stock BCD width,
  odd/even powers, reversed multiplication, constant-expression powers,

  Added focused non-power rejection tests for division, remainder, and compound
