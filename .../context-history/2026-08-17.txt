2026-08-17 09:23:41 PDT, expand Tanks to 16-way movement
Tanks now uses sixteen 22.5-degree headings. N, NNE, and NE are the
canonical glyphs; the remaining thirteen are rotations/reflections. Motion
accounts for doubled arena Y with half-rate diagonals plus 13/16 and inverse
3/16 intermediate cadences, giving distinct screen-space trajectories without

existing bounded octant search after rounding the missile
heading to the nearest octant. The emulator oracle now dynamically proves NNE
and ENE diverge, while raster, collision, audio, spin, and barrier behavior stay
stable.
