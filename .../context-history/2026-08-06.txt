2026-08-06 08:59:46 PDT, completed bankswitching roadmap item 29

Added explicit optimized and simple bank-placement modes, keeping optimized as
the default. The optimized mode now uses deterministic byte/cycle/site-weighted
component ordering and a fixed-point single-component local pass which preserves
all hard components, pins, replica locality, and capacity constraints and rejects
moves that increase weighted hardware-return depth. Added
--explain-bank-placement candidate, rejection, move, and final-metric tracing;
map output records the selected mode.

Added focused placement fixtures. Simple versus optimized measures two JSR
bridges versus one and weighted depth 6 versus 5 in the ordering case; four
bridges versus one with depth held at 5 in the local-move case; and a tempting
60-to-30 byte-weight move is rejected because weighted depth would rise from 5
to 7. The maintained standard-renderer F8/F6/F4 images remain byte-identical
between modes because their architectural split is hard-pinned. Reclassified
roadmap item 30 as a mapper-family policy gate rather than unfinished work.

source-hygiene regressions pass, and staged installed-toolchain/installcheck
