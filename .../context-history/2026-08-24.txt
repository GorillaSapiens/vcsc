2026-08-24 21:28:26 PDT, Tanks missile power-on latch fix
Investigated a Stella 7.0/Windows report where P0 fire made its first sound but
M0 never appeared and later fire presses were suppressed, while P1 and Stella
8.0/Linux behaved normally. A clean Stella 7.0/Linux player profile showed M0,
but Stella developer TIA randomization reproduced the missing left missile.

The Tanks startup initialized ENAMx, HMMx, NUSIZx, and other beam state but had
never cleared RESMP0/RESMP1. A nonzero RESMP0 leaves M0 locked to P0: launch
sets missile0_active and starts audio, but the independently positioned missile
is hidden/attached, so the active latch can prevent later launches. A controlled
Stella 7 probe that seeded RESMP0=2 after startup reproduced the missing M0;
seeding the same dirty state before the corrected startup initialization leaves
M0 visible.

Tanks now explicitly clears both RESMP0 and RESMP1 before initial missile
positioning. vcs_tanks.pl pins that startup invariant so neither reset-to-player
latch can become an implicit power-on-state dependency again. Validation:
vcs_tanks.pl, example_assembly_allowlist.pl, source_tree_hygiene.pl, and
context_history_format.pl pass; example build/round-trip shard 5/8 containing
Tanks also passes; the Stella 7 dirty-RESMP0 repair probe shows the P0 missile.
