2026-07-18 16:01:27 PDT, shorten scalar return epilogues for VCS zero page

  one-byte operand is emitted as a low-byte relocation rather than an invalid
  word relocation.
* Added linked VCS-byte regression coverage for the exact `A5 zp 60` and
  `A5 zp A6 zp+1 60` encodings; updated source-code generation expectations.
