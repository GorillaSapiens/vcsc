2026-07-17 15:31:37 PDT, lifetime-pooled scratch and common indexed address reuse
* Replaced the per-site temporary-symbol families with depth-indexed
  `__n65_scratch_N` pools scoped to each function/translation-unit context.
  Sequential expressions reuse a slot, nested expressions receive deeper slots,
  and caller/callee functions retain separate physical storage.
* Added explicit acquire/activate/deactivate/release leases, LIFO validation, and
  an end-of-compilation check that rejects unreleased scratch. Each pooled slot
  is emitted once at its maximum observed size.
* Kept scratch leases live until final copy-out. Execution testing caught and
  fixed an early-release bug where recomputing a computed destination address
  could overwrite the pending RHS value; the same lifetime rule was applied to
  cast, operator, braced, compound, and increment/decrement copy-out paths.
* Added safe common-address lowering for two binary operands that are direct
  fields of the same runtime-indexed aggregate element. Identical index trees
  compute ptr0 once and load both fields by offsets; differing indices and
  ambiguous pointer/bitfield/ref/conversion cases retain independent lowering.
* Added layout, address-count, and execution regressions covering sequential
  reuse, nested separation, caller/callee isolation, computed assignments,
  braced and compound stores, increment/decrement, and shared indexed fields.
* Passed 223/223 compile tests and 94/94 end-to-end tests; both examples remain
  exact 4096-byte cartridges, and Ode passes 1497 consecutive Stella-reported
  262-line frames.
* No files were removed; remove.txt remains at 462 unique cumulative paths.
