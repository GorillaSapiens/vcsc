VCSC SPECIALIZATION / INLINING -- DURABLE CLOSEOUT
==================================================

Status
------
No unfinished roadmap items. Completed implementation notes and measured examples
are archived in `.../context-history/`; the full pre-cleanup roadmap is in
2026-08-22.

Durable policy
--------------
* Safe single-callsite `ref` and readonly by-value parameter specialization is
  part of normal compilation when identity/aliasing/stability proofs succeed.
* Optimizer-selected ordinary-function IR inlining is not source-text substitution
  and is separate from source `inline` semantics.
* Placement/timing contracts, ABI/export identity, assembly-visible identity,
  lifecycle hooks, and independently placeable regions are legality gates.
* Whole-program measured inlining remains opt-in through `-finline-profit`; it
  uses final link measurements, rejects ROM growth, and treats unexpected RAM
  overlay loss as an allocator/lifetime bug rather than a normal tradeoff.
* Reachability/DCE must preserve runtime initializer roots and must not hide source
  or assembler diagnostics.

Future optimization work should be opened as a new concrete roadmap item instead
of appending historical experiments here.
