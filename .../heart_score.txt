HEART SCORE COMPONENT
=====================

Stable state
------------
`libraries/vcs/heart_score_component.c26` is the maintained fixed-footprint
0..11.5-heart component. `score` is the full-heart count (clamped at 11),
`half` adds the right half of the next slot, and `color` selects the heart color.
The 11 full slots are fixed at TIA X=16,28,...,136; smaller values are exact
left prefixes and never move an already-visible heart.

All 24 visible states execute from hard-page ROM renderers. The 12 full-heart
renderers are the exact 45-byte prepatched Jentzsch streams. `0.5` is a single
P0 right-half sprite at slot zero. `1.5` through `11.5` use Ball plus M0: Ball
supplies the variable-width left edge of the right half and M0 supplies its
fixed two-clock strip. From three full hearts onward M0's first repeated copy is
buried entirely inside lit pixels of the preceding full heart and its second
copy forms the new half heart.

Ball/M0 positioning uses the maintained divide-by-15 RESP/HMxx algorithm at its
correct WSYNC phase: the score clamp/table lookup occurs before WSYNC, then the
post-WSYNC sequence matches the standard positioner. Do not move that lookup
back after WSYNC; doing so reintroduces coarse-position discontinuities.

One instance uses 8 RIOT-RAM bytes total: public `score`, `half`, and `color`, a
two-byte ROM-renderer pointer, and three cached TIA setup bytes. It consumes 7
visible scanlines. Full 0..11 Stella hashes are unchanged from the full-heart
ROM implementation. Independent pixel-oracle validation proves each half state
is exactly N full hearts plus the 32-pixel right half of the next slot, with no
stray M0/Ball pixels.

Half states use M0, Ball, `COLUPF`, and the Ball-width bits of `CTRLPF`; M0 and
Ball are disabled on return but their horizontal geometry is not restored. A
following gameplay renderer that owns those objects must reposition them.

Next work
