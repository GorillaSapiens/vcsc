HEART SCORE COMPONENT
=====================

Maintained implementation
-------------------------
`libraries/vcs/heart_score_component.c26` is the fixed-footprint 0..11-heart
component. `score` is the full-heart count, `half` adds the left half of the next
heart through 10.5, and score 11 ignores `half`. The 11 slots remain fixed at
TIA X=16,28,...,136 with 8-pixel hearts on a 12-pixel pitch.

`boxes` is an independent 0..11 capacity and `box_color` is one configurable
color shared by every enabled box. Boxes are 8-pixel playfield rectangles at the
same 11 slots. VBLANK clamps health to capacity, including clearing a half-heart
that would extend past the final box. The playfield uses fixed-cycle asymmetric
PF1/PF2 transitions; only byte values vary with capacity.

Full-heart and low/mid half-heart paths preserve the established player timing.
Dense 8.5, 9.5, and 10.5 paths are unrolled so the SAX half-heart write and PF
transition both fit without moving heart pixels. P0/P1 positioning is owned by
`draw()`, not inherited from VBLANK. The component still consumes seven visible
scanlines. It now owns PF0/PF1/PF2, COLUPF, and reflected CTRLPF while drawing
and uses 16 RIOT-RAM bytes per instance. Terminal cleanup clears PF0/PF1/PF2 but
does not restore COLUPF/CTRLPF.

Stella 7.0 preserves all historical heart-only hashes with box color hidden and
separately locks visible box geometry for every capacity 0..11. The interactive
above/below composition examples are F8: application/gameplay remains in bank0;
the heart dispatcher and renderer targets live in bank1 (`banked:=1`). Right
joystick UP/DOWN changes health by half-heart, RIGHT/LEFT increases/decreases
box capacity, left joystick moves the selected P0/P1/Ball, and SELECT cycles the
selection. The score-above gap re-establishes the following gameplay renderer's
COLUPF/CTRLPF because the heart component legitimately owns those registers.
