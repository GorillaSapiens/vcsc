HEART SCORE COMPONENT -- PAUSED WIP
===================================

Goal
----
Add a reusable score component, analogous to the maintained score components,
that displays 0..11 Zelda-style hearts. Eleven hearts define a centered fixed
footprint; smaller values are left-justified prefixes of that same footprint.
An optional final half-heart is desirable, but it must not block correct 0..11
full-heart support.

Authoritative technique
-----------------------
Thomas Jentzsch's supplied 11-Invaders binary is the hardware oracle. It already
handles populations 1..11 with one fixed-cycle self-modifying RIOT-RAM renderer
plus per-count setup/patching. Do not special-case 1..3 and do not redesign the
renderer from partial timing observations. Port the complete per-count setup and
renderer behavior first, replace only the invader bitmap with the heart bitmap,
and validate all 12 full-heart states before attempting a ROM-frozen variant or
half-heart enhancement.

Current unfinished files
------------------------
* `libraries/vcs/heart_score_component.c26` -- experimental ROM-specialized
  component; not validated and not release-ready.
* `heart_ram_prefix_component.c26` -- experimental literal RAM renderer
  adaptation; currently the closer direction but still incomplete.
* `heart_prefix_component.c26`, `hprefix_probe.c26`, `hram_probe.c26` -- bench
  prototypes/probes retained for continuation.

Next step
---------
Resume from the complete Jentzsch per-count setup + RAM renderer, not the
specialized prototypes. Prove 0..11 full hearts in Stella with the 11-heart row
centered and every smaller count occupying the identical left-edge prefix. Only
then wrap the proven implementation in the ordinary component contract/example
and investigate a half-heart separately.
