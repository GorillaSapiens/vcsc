HEART SCORE COMPONENT
=====================

Maintained implementation
-------------------------
`libraries/vcs/heart_score_component.c26` is the fixed-footprint 0..11-heart
component. `score` is the full-heart count (clamped at 11), `half` adds the left
half of the next heart through 10.5, and `color` selects the heart color. At
score 11, `half` is ignored visually. The 11 full slots remain fixed at TIA
X=16,28,...,136; smaller values are exact left prefixes.

The 12 full renderers remain the certified 45-byte Jentzsch streams. Half state
0 uses a P0 high-nibble glyph. Half states 1..9 preserve Jentzsch row timing and
use SAX with X=$F0 to write only the high nibble into the next player copy.
10.5 is an unrolled six-row renderer because its loop has no spare four-cycle
slot. The half-control renderer for score 11 draws exactly the normal 11-heart
maximum, so no 11.5 geometry is required.

The dispatcher cycle-balances X selection: full renderers enter with X=0; half
renderers enter with X=$F0. Half pixels use P0/P1 only. The old Ball/M0 overlay,
position tables, and per-row Ball motion are gone. VBLANK still zeroes missile
and Ball motion registers before global HMOVE setup so unrelated objects are not
displaced.

One instance uses 8 RIOT-RAM bytes total: public `score`, `half`, and `color`, a
two-byte renderer pointer, and three cached TIA setup bytes. It consumes seven
visible scanlines. Stella 7.0 locks all 23 visible values plus the score-11 half
clamp. Independent image comparison proves every 0.5..10.5 raster equals the
unchanged N-heart raster plus exactly the left half of heart N+1; all twelve full
hashes remain unchanged.

`examples/01_basic/14_heart_score` also contains 4K interactive composition
cartridges with the maintained 181-line player-color renderer both below and
above the heart meter. Four blank lines complete the 192-line visible field.
Right joystick UP/DOWN changes health in half-heart steps across 0..11, with a
neutral-required edge latch. Stella snapshots lock both composition orders and
one UP/DOWN transition from the initial 5.5 state.
