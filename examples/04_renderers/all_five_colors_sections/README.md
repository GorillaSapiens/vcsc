# All-five row colours and sections — experimental preview

[`no_score`](no_score/) is a runnable NTSC preview combining independent
eight-row player colours with continuous playfield/background sections.

**This is not the finished configurable renderer.** Its six seam positions are
fixed at scanlines 18, 20, 22, 24, 26 and 28. General eight-slot geometry and the
combined score-above/score-below examples remain unfinished.
